# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Listeners using youworship to browse worship songs, play tracks, and open lyrics during personal listening, practice, or devotional time.

## Product Purpose
youworship is a music app for discovering and playing Christian worship songs with lyrics-first browsing and a calm listening workflow.

## Brand Personality
Premium, reverent, focused. The product should feel like a polished music app with quiet devotional cues rather than a church flyer or generic media player.

## Anti-references
Avoid overly religious decoration, muddy icon artwork, stock church imagery, generic Spotify cloning, and loud gradient-heavy branding.

## Design Principles
- Lead with music-app clarity: browsing, search, playback, and lyrics should feel immediate.
- Keep the worship identity subtle and respectful.
- Use a restrained dark product shell with crisp hierarchy and high contrast.
- Make brand details feel intentional at small sizes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target readable contrast across dark and light themes. Keep controls recognizable, keyboard-focusable, and tolerant of reduced motion preferences.
